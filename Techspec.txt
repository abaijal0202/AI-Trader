Executive Summary

761999127303



This Business Requirements Document (BRD) defines the scope, architecture, and constraints for developing a sophisticated, compliance-first Agentic AI Trading System designed for the Indian retail trading market. The system leverages a multi-agent architecture powered by Ollama&apos;s local Large Language Models (LLMs) to ensure low latency and data privacy. It strictly integrates with the ICICI Direct Breeze API, adhering to SEBI&apos;s algorithmic trading rules slated for 2025-2026, specifically regarding API execution limits, static IPs, and algorithmic identifiers.








Business Context and Objectives

761999117514



The objective is to democratize institutional-grade trading intelligence for retail quantitative traders while maintaining rigorous adherence to emerging Indian regulatory frameworks. The system aims to automate technical analysis, signal generation, news sentiment processing, and order execution without sacrificing pre-trade risk controls.

System Architecture

761999117780



The target architecture is a modular, multi-agent system coordinated by an orchestration layer and backed by deterministic execution controls. Language-model reasoning is hosted locally through Ollama to reduce sensitive data exposure, improve latency, and preserve operability during internet instability. No LLM output may directly place trades; all trade actions must pass through typed tool/function calls, schema validation, rule validation, and risk approval gates.








Architecture Principles







Local-first intelligence: Run LLM inference on Ollama within the controlled network boundary.


Deterministic execution: Convert agent outputs to structured JSON commands only after schema validation.


Separation of duties: Keep signal generation, risk approval, and execution as separate runtime responsibilities.


Compliance by design: Embed OPS throttling, limit-order enforcement, audit logging, and policy checks centrally.


Fail-safe behavior: On data gaps, authentication failures, or abnormal volatility, default to no-trade mode.

Trading Strategies

761999127300



The platform shall support configurable strategy templates rather than hard-coded one-off rules. Each strategy must have parameter sets, approval status, risk profile, and deployment mode (disabled, backtest, paper, pilot live, production live).





Strategy


Business Intent


System Logic


Key Guardrails


Day Trading - Momentum / Mean Reversion


Capture intraday directional or reversal moves in liquid cash equities or index derivatives.


Use RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, moving average alignment, volume filters, and time-window filters; mandatory intraday square-off.


High liquidity universe, no overnight carry, max trades per symbol, no trading near close unless policy allows.


F&amp;O Straddle


Express volatility view around events or exploit premium decay depending on long/short configuration.


Construct ATM call and put combinations; evaluate IV regime, theta decay, event risk, and margin sufficiency.


Lot-size validation, event whitelist, defined stop-loss or max loss, exposure cap by expiry.


F&amp;O Strangle


Trade wider expected move ranges while reducing premium or directional sensitivity compared with straddles.


Select OTM strikes based on volatility bands, ATR, implied volatility, and target premium constraints.


Strike distance rules, slippage controls, margin threshold, early exit rules on volatility expansion.


Pairs Trading / Statistical Arbitrage


Monetize temporary divergence between historically related instruments.


Use spread normalization, correlation / cointegration screening, z-score thresholds, and reversion exit logic.


Only approved pairs, hedge ratio validation, maximum holding period, spread stop-loss, liquidity screens.
